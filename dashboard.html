Methodology & Data Notes

How this dashboard is built

Data. 10,000 patient-level admissions () with demographics, condition, admission/discharge dates, billing, admission type, insurer, medication and test results. Length of stay = discharge date − admission date.

KPIs & scorecard. pandas group-by aggregation. A condition's status is the sum of z-scores for stay length, billing and abnormal-result rate relative to the six conditions (Watch > 0.5, Needs Attention > 1.5).

Forecast. Ordinary least-squares regression (scikit-learn) on monthly admission counts, projected 6 months. Partial first/last months are excluded so they don't bias the slope.

Decision engine. Rules flag high-deviation conditions, above-average Emergency share, and payers whose average billing exceeds the network mean by >1%.

Caveat. This is the widely used synthetic "Healthcare Dataset": values are randomly generated, so metrics cluster near the average and the forecast is flat (low R²). The pipeline will surface sharper signals on a real EHR/ADT extract. Reproduce all numbers with python src/healthcare_ops_analytics.py.
